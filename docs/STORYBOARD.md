# STORYBOARD (SB)

Six short acts plus a lock. Target: about 4 to 6 minutes start to letter, then as long as she wants in "stay a while". Every act can be replayed.

**Act 0. The lock** (until 10 Nov 00:00 IST). A frosted window or snow globe, the countdown, soft snow, maybe a tiny sleeping shape inside. At zero it opens. The owner's words, not Claude's.

**Act 1. The sleepy nook.** Warm cabin interior, blue snow outside. Chamgadad is asleep under a blanket, the cat curled on its feet, cocoa steaming, the lamp low. A tiny hint: "hold to wake Chamgadad". Holding raises the light slowly. If left alone, she sleeps deeper and something sweet happens (the "let her sleep" secret). *Beat:* she stirs, stretches, sees you, her eyes find your finger.

**Act 2. The door.** Chamgadad waves you along, opens the door; the camera pushes out into the snow; the aurora is already waking. *Beat:* the first footsteps in snow, the hush, the music box not yet.

**Act 3. Names in the sky.** The meadow at blue hour. Faint stars hint at shapes. She (the viewer) drags to connect each one: **Strawberry**, **Bumblebee**, **Rose** (a single frost-rose), **Kaaju Katli** (a diamond), **Cutie Patootie** (a cat-and-bunny shape). Each completion: a chime, a bloom of light, Chamgadad reacts, and one wish card appears (the owner's words). The snow bunny peeks between constellations; the cat follows. Order is free; hints appear if she stalls.

**Act 4. The cake.** A snow-table with a small snow-dusted cake appears under the pines. "Make a wish." She blows on her phone (mic) or taps the candles. They flicker and go out one by one. *Beat:* quiet, then the lanterns release.

**Act 5. The letter.** A lantern drifts down carrying an envelope (alternative: the envelope on the table; the owner chooses). She opens it. The letter, exactly as the owner wrote it, in a calm reading view. A quiet sign-off.

**Act 6. The finale.** The five constellations lift and join into one message across the sky; the aurora swells; the music-box "Happy Birthday" plays softly; the camera pulls back through the pines to the cabin glowing in the valley. Then **stay a while**: wander, pet the animals, replay a constellation, re-read a wish, find secrets.

## Cast
Chamgadad (her drawing, in 3D, the host of the card) · the cat · the snow bunny · optional kiwi-bird cameo (owner's call) · the moon (it wears a party hat if tapped five times).

## Rules
- Nothing is a game over. Nothing can be failed.
- Every interaction has a tap or keyboard path.
- Sound is off until her first tap, always optional, mute always visible.
- The letter is never skipped, never auto-advanced.
