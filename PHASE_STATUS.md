### Phase 8 — Ear motion and secondary animation (2026-11-05, done in the sandbox)
- Dependency check: Phase 8 depends on Phase 6, which is ✅ (sandbox). Phase 7's mouth line is still open (Q1) but Phase 8 does not need it.
- Design choices, with reasons: (a) **implicit (backward Euler) springs with sub-steps of at most 1/120 s and a 0.1 s frame clamp**, because the phase asks for "no explosion at large dt" and an implicit step cannot gain energy at any step size; explicit Euler would need a stability limit per spring. (b) The ears react to the head's **own-axis rotation and sideways move per frame** (not to a velocity estimate), so there is nothing to differentiate and a paused loop gives no kick. A teleport is clamped (0.5 rad, 1 world unit per frame) and `resync()` forgets the last transform. (c) **Swing about the ear's pivot (z), small depth tilts (x, y), and the fold as a second spring** on the bowl about the same pivot, driven by the opposite of the ear's swing, so the fold seems to bend a frame behind the ear. The phase says "the upright ear's fold flexing"; both ears are drawn folded in her art (Phase 6), so the angled ear flexes at half strength `[A]`. (d) The flick moves the swing **target** (rise 0.05 s, fall 0.16 s) instead of kicking the velocity, so its size is predictable (tests) and the spring supplies the overshoot. The direction is outward and up per ear, worked out from the ear's measured axis, never into the head. (e) **Low tier = sine sway** (`earModeForTier('low')`), as the phase asks; it ignores the head. (f) Reduced motion keeps a smaller tug (0.4) so the ears are not dead, but nothing moves on its own.

### Phase 10 — Rig and behaviour set (2026-11-05, done in the sandbox)
- Dependency check: Phase 10 depends on 8 and 9, both ✅ (sandbox). No owner input needed, so I did not ask for any: the phase text's "head tilt from the drawing" is the head as Phase 6 traced it, so `rest` is the Phase 9 `sit` pose and `?behave&still&at=0` is pixel-identical to `?body`.
- Design choices, with reasons: (a) **a small procedural rig of the Phase 9 pivot groups, not a skin** (the meshes are rigid inflated parts, and the outline shader does not support skinning yet): states are poses as data and the machine blends between them. (b) **Two layers:** one posture or one-shot at a time, and face gestures (slow blink, ear flick) that never change the posture, so a blink during a lean needs no new state. (c) **Interruption safety by snapshot:** every change blends from the pose on screen, never from the old state's idea of itself; one-shots (`wake`, `hop`, `wave`) have a free point before which a new request waits in a one-slot queue, latest wins; asking for anything while asleep routes through `wake`. (d) **Lazy profile:** smootherstep everywhere, no overshoot, long holds; eyes and Zs follow targets with their own half-lives (0.3 s, 0.9 s) so the eyes lead and the body follows, the pointer is smoothed for the head (0.28 s) while the eyes read it directly; blend time grows with travel (0.6 s per radian, capped at 2.6 s). (e) **Breath is one phase accumulator** whose rate and size are blended, so changing state never jumps the chest; the rig scales the torso about the neck joint and lifts the whole figure by what the scale took away, so the seat stays on the ground. (f) **Reduced motion** is part of the machine, not a CSS afterthought: scaled poses, slower blends, substitutions (`hop` to `curiousLean`, `wake` to a plain slow blend to rest). (g) The rig owns the face and the ear driver, and calls them in a fixed order after the pose is written, so the ears see the head's real motion for the first time.
